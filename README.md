Customer Churn Analysis using Power BI :

Customer churn is one of the most critical challenges faced by subscription-based businesses, especially in the telecom industry. This project focuses on analyzing customer churn patterns to understand why customers leave, identify high-risk segments, and provide data-driven recommendations to improve customer retention and long-term profitability.

The analysis is performed using Power BI, leveraging interactive dashboards and advanced visualizations to uncover key churn drivers.

 Business Objective

To help the company understand customer behavior better and design effective retention strategies to reduce churn and improve long-term profitability.

 Key Insights

New customers (0–12 months tenure) have the highest churn rate.

Month-to-month contracts show significantly higher churn than long-term contracts.

Fiber optic users experience the highest churn among internet service types.

Customers with fewer subscribed services are more likely to churn.

Electronic check payment users show higher churn compared to auto-payment users.

Bundled services increase customer stickiness and reduce churn.

Senior citizens show slightly higher churn and may benefit from targeted retention offers.

 Business Recommendations

Focus on early retention for customers in their first year.

Promote long-term contracts with discounts and loyalty benefits.

Improve fiber optic service quality to reduce dissatisfaction.

Encourage automatic payment methods to lower churn.

Use churn risk scores to proactively retain high-risk customers.
